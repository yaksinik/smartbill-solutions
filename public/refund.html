Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2026

At SmartBill, we strive for customer satisfaction. Please read our refund policy carefully.

1. Eligibility for Refunds

Refunds are generally available for accidental overpayments or duplicate transactions. Requests must be made within 7 days of the transaction.

2. Process

To request a refund, please contact us at yaksinik@gmail.com with your transaction ID and the reason for the request. We will review and process eligible requests within 5-7 business days.

3. Razorpay Fees

Please note that Razorpay transaction fees may not be refundable in all cases, depending on the circumstances of the refund.
